How this index works

Each card below is one country. A Country badge means a full paint-ready flag timeline exists, newest flag first, with an internal-regions map of that country's own historically distinct areas. A Hub badge means the country appears as a real, colored region in a hub's cross-country map and timeline, but doesn't have its own dedicated flag-by-flag page yet — the card links to that hub instead.

The crown/empire tags under each card are the powers that controlled that country for some real stretch of its history, drawn from the hub maps' own era data — not exhaustive, but accurate to what's actually been built so far. They're searchable too: try typing "Ottoman" or "Habsburg" into the box above.
